Customer Purchase Behavior Analytics

Built a multi-page merchandise performance and sales intelligence dashboard in Microsoft Power BI using cleaned wholesale transactional datasets from 2024–2026 to analyze sales revenue, inventory movement, merchandise category performance, reorder behavior, customer demand trends, forecasting, and sales operations KPIs for data-driven merchandising and business planning.

Utilized Power BI features including drill-through navigation, forecasting analytics, interactive slicers, cross-filtering visuals, DAX measures, KPI cards, Top-N analysis, dynamic filtering, Power Query transformations, custom data modeling, and executive-level dashboard storytelling to support operational and strategic decision-making.
